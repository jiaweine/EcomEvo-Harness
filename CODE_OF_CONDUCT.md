# Community Code of Conduct

EcomEvo welcomes respectful technical collaboration across experience levels, organizations, languages, and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable, technically specific feedback.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and responsible disclosure.
- Credit prior work and make conflicts of interest clear.
- Help keep issues and reviews focused on reproducible product outcomes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, credential disclosure, and publication of private customer or security data are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, restrict participation, or report abuse to the hosting platform when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled with discretion and only shared as needed for investigation and response.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related public or private communication.
